# Privacy

This blog can use **Google Analytics 4** for aggregate traffic and acquisition analytics and **Microsoft Clarity** for heatmaps and anonymous session analysis. Both services are optional and remain disabled until you explicitly accept analytics.

The blog does not intentionally send names, email addresses, authenticated identities, or other directly identifying information to analytics services. Test and production sites use separate analytics configurations.

You can accept or reject analytics in the consent banner. To change your choice later, use the **Analytics preferences** link in the footer when available, or clear this site's local storage and reload the page.
